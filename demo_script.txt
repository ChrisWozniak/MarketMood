MARKET MOOD — DEMO SCRIPT (FIRST DRAFT)
========================================
Total target time: 2:15 – 3:15


────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1: PROBLEM & ROOT CAUSE
Target: 30–45 seconds
────────────────────────────────────────

Marcus is a retail investor.

Every morning he checks Reddit, scrolls finance Twitter, skims
HackerNews, watches CNBC — then tries to decide:

  "Is the mood out there actually bullish or bearish right now?"

The problem isn't a lack of information.
It's that the signal is buried in noise across a dozen platforms —
and by the time Marcus pieces it together, the moment has passed.

The root cause:
No tool connects what people are SAYING to what Marcus should be WATCHING.

Sentiment tools, market signals, and tech momentum all live in
separate silos.

Marcus is doing the synthesis himself — every day — manually.


────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2: SOLUTION & DEMO
Target: 90–120 seconds
────────────────────────────────────────

Market Mood is a real-time social sentiment barometer that reads
the crowd — Reddit, HackerNews, YouTube, prediction markets, and
economic data — then uses AI to turn that mood into investment
signals, all in one dashboard.

--- SHOW: Dashboard ---

At a glance, Marcus sees where sentiment is hot and where it's
cooling — across 9 categories: Economy, Crypto, Tech, Real Estate,
and more.

--- SHOW: Settings > Custom Watchlist ---

Marcus types in his tickers: NVDA, BTC, ETH.

Now the analysis isn't generic — it's personal to what he owns.

--- CLICK: Run Now ---

The AI is pulling posts from Reddit, HackerNews, YouTube —
cross-referencing Kalshi and Polymarket prediction markets,
layering in FRED housing data —
then Gemini synthesizes it all.

--- SHOW: Results — Barometer rows ---

Here's the Economy bar. One of today's top subtopics:
"Fed holds rates steady at 4.25%"

--- SHOW: Custom Watchlist row ---

And Marcus's own tickers — right there in the barometer.
Highlighted in amber so he can't miss them.

--- SHOW: Investment Signals ---

Gemini says: bullish on NVDA.
Here's why — based on today's tech sentiment and prediction markets.

--- SHOW: Email ---

And Marcus got all of this delivered to his inbox —
same analysis, formatted report, every morning automatically.


────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3: WHAT'S NEXT
Target: 15–30 seconds
────────────────────────────────────────

The one feature I'd build next: sentiment shift alerts.

Right now Marcus has to open the app to check.
But the most valuable moment is when something CHANGES FAST —

  Crypto sentiment swings from +40 to –60 in two hours.
  A prediction market moves 20 points overnight.

The next step is pushing that signal to Marcus BEFORE he even
thinks to look — via text or push notification.

That's what turns Market Mood from a dashboard into an
early warning system.
