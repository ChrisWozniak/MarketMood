# Market Mood — MVP Presentation Script

---

Hi everyone, my name is Christopher, and I'm the builder behind Market Mood.

Let me introduce you to Marcus. He's a trader — not a Wall Street professional, just someone who takes investing seriously and wants to make smarter decisions with his money.

Every morning Marcus wakes up and does the same exhausting thing. He checks Reddit. Then Twitter. Then YouTube. Then a prediction market. Then a financial news site. Then another one. By the time he's done, an hour has passed, he's more confused than when he started, and the market is already moving without him.

That's where Market Mood comes in.

Market Mood is a real-time trading intelligence dashboard that does everything Marcus was doing manually — in one place, automatically, powered by Google Gemini. It pulls live sentiment data from Reddit, Hacker News, YouTube, Kalshi, and Polymarket — nine topic categories, five data sources, one AI reasoning pass — and delivers clear, actionable investment signals before Marcus has finished his coffee.

Let me show you how it works.

---

**(Open app — confirm all accordion tabs are closed)**

When you first set up Market Mood, you start here — in Settings. This is where Marcus builds his personal watchlist, selects the topic categories he cares about, and sets how often the system runs. Onboarding takes two minutes. After that, Market Mood runs hourly on its own.

---

**(Navigate to dashboard)**

This is the main dashboard. Everything Marcus used to check separately — sentiment scores, prediction market probabilities, macroeconomic indicators — is fused into one AI reasoning pass right here.

Not siloed across five tabs. One view. One signal.

---

**(Open first accordion)**

Let's look at sentiment by category. Market Mood is reading nine topic areas in real time — from tech and crypto to housing and energy. Each one gets a sentiment score and a directional signal. Green means the crowd is bullish. Red means caution. The AI isn't just aggregating — it's interpreting.

---

**(Open watchlist)**

And here's Marcus's personal watchlist. These are the specific assets he told Market Mood to track. When sentiment shifts on something he's watching, Market Mood flags it — so he's reacting to signals, not noise.

---

**(Reference email digest)**

And after every scheduled run, Market Mood delivers a clean HTML email digest straight to his inbox. He wakes up, opens one email, and knows exactly where attention is needed. No more tab switching. No more information overload.

That's what turns Market Mood from a customized economy dashboard into a money-making alarm system.

---

Marcus gets his hour back every morning. He trades with clarity instead of confusion. He never misses a signal again.

That's Market Mood. Thank you.
